Project Cost Report.

Track project costs, income received and the net financial balance.

Calculation: Project cost = labour attendance earnings + recorded expenses. Net balance = income received − project cost. Salary withdrawals and salary payments are excluded because they settle labour already counted from attendance. Inventory quantities are not included in rupee totals because the current stock ledger has no material purchase-price valuation.
